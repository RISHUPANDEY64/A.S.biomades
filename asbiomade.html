ABOUT THE FACILITY

Built for brands that outgrew their first manufacturer.

A.S. Biomeds started as a two-room lab formulating for regional D2C skincare brands. Today the facility runs full-batch production for personal care, haircare, grooming and baby-care lines — while keeping the thing brands actually complain about elsewhere: a formulator who answers the phone.

Every project moves through the same in-house chain — R&D, stability testing, pilot batch, bulk manufacturing and packaging — so nothing is handed off to a third party mid-way, and nothing about your formula leaves the building.

  • R&D Senior formulators and quality specialists oversee every batch, from first sample to final testing
  • ISO 22716 Good Manufacturing Practice for cosmetics, audited annually
  • GMP certified production lines for skin, hair and baby-care categories
  • 3,000+ raw ingredients on file, including natural and Ayurvedic actives
  • NDA-first every enquiry is covered before formulation details are shared

HOW A PROJECT MOVES

From brief to dispatch, in six stages.

01

Brief & NDA

You share the product brief, target price point and expected volume; we sign an NDA before any formulation talk starts.

02

Formulation

Our R&D team builds the formula from your brief or reference, choosing ingredients within your target cost band.

03

Sampling

You receive lab samples for feedback — texture, fragrance and performance are refined here before anything scales up.

04

Stability testing

The approved formula is tested across temperature and time to confirm shelf life before bulk manufacturing begins.

05

Bulk manufacturing

Production runs at your confirmed batch size, under the same GMP line used for the pilot batch.

06

Packaging & dispatch

Filling, labelling and carton packing in-house, then handover to your logistics partner or ours.
